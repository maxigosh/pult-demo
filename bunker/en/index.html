03 · COMMUNICATION

The agent drafts your reply

Every client has their own AI agent that knows the whole history with them.

  • Reads the client card, past orders, tasks and conversations
  • Offers 1–3 options and explains the purpose of each
  • “Redo with a comment” gives you a new option
 Agent: draft a reply · Kittiwake Shipping

3With an alternative
“Good afternoon! I’ll send the Fujairah quote within the hour. If it suits you better, we can also look at delivery in Khor Fakkan.”
why: gives the client a choice of port

Nothing goes out without your “yes”

The send gate: the agent suggests, you send.

  • Exactly the approved text goes out, and only once
  • Limits per chat and account, pauses between messages
  • Approve from Telegram by replying “1”, “2” or “3”
