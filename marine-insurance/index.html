02 · SALES

Law of large numbers: a touch a day

Sales is a numbers game. At 100 attempts the result is random; at 10,000 it becomes predictable. The Pult keeps a personal conversation going with every owner, every day, without a call-centre.

  • A cadence of touches per owner: news, renewal reminder, market update, a call
  • Each message is personal: the agent knows the fleet, the club, the last talk
  • One “yes” approves a whole list; messages go one by one with pauses, in working hours

09 · KNOWLEDGE

The common language of marine insurance

Like aviation, marine insurance has a language understood in every port: few words, clear symbols. The agent speaks it, so your team and your clients do too. Tap a term.

MIA 1906
🚢
H&M
Hull & Machinery
Cover for the ship itself: hull, engine, equipment, against physical damage and total loss.
IG clubs
🤝
P&I
Protection & Indemnity
Liability to third parties: crew, cargo, pollution, ports, wreck removal. Mostly mutual clubs.
Ch. 4
⚖️
General Average
shared sacrifice
A loss made on purpose to save ship and cargo is shared by all interests (York-Antwerp Rules).
Ch. 4
🔧
Particular Average
partial loss
Partial damage that falls on one interest only, paid by its own insurer.
Ch. 4
⚓
TLO
Total Loss Only
Pays only if the ship is lost or a constructive total loss; often for older tonnage.
Ch. 2
📝
Slip
the placing document
The broker’s summary of the risk; underwriters write their lines on it, lead first, then followers.
Ch. 1
🎯
Lead / Follow
subscription market
The lead sets the terms; followers take shares (lines) on the same terms.
Ch. 4
💲
Deductible
the owner’s share
The part of each claim the owner pays first. Higher deductible, lower premium.
Ch. 10
💰
IV
Increased Value
Extra cover above the hull value, so a total loss pays out the full value of the ship.
Ch. 10
🛡️
War Risks
listed areas
Excluded from hull cover; written separately, with additional premium for listed areas.
Ch. 5
🚨
Sue & Labour
act as if uninsured
The owner must act to reduce a loss; reasonable costs are paid by insurers on top.
Ch. 5
🛟
Salvage
reward for saving
Payment to those who save property at sea; recoverable from insurers.
Ch. 6
💥
Collision · ¾ RDC
running down clause
Hull insurers pay three-fourths of collision liability; P&I takes the other quarter.
Ch. 3
🤲
Utmost Good Faith
duty of fair presentation
Both sides disclose what matters to the risk. The base of every placement.
Ch. 5
🔁
Subrogation
insurer steps in
After paying, the insurer takes the owner’s rights to recover from the party at fault.
IG clubs
📅
Certificate of Entry
P&I membership · 20 Feb
Proof that the ship is entered in a club; most clubs renew on 20 February.
Based on the Institute of Chartered Shipbrokers course “Marine Insurance”: the Marine Insurance Act 1906, Institute clauses, York-Antwerp Rules.
